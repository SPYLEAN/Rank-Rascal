# Rascal Realms: Crownfall
## Release 1 Production Specification
### Chapter 1 — The Sign That Lied

**Status:** Working baseline for Release 1\
**Phase:** Pre-production / Resource Foundation\
**Target platform:** Roblox\
**Game type:** Story-driven open-zone co-op action RPG mystery\
**Release philosophy:** **Small first chapter. Ridiculous polish. Obvious future.**

This document is the canonical working scope for Release 1. It exists to prevent scope drift and to give ChatGPT, Claude, Antigravity, artists, developers, animators, and future contributors a shared build reference.

Use these status labels:
- **LOCKED** — core canon or release direction.
- **APPROVED** — accepted production direction.
- **PLANNED** — intended but not yet finalized.
- **CONCEPT** — being explored.
- **DEFERRED** — intentionally moved beyond Release 1.
- **CUT** — removed from current scope.
- **N/A** — not applicable.

When a future idea conflicts with this document, do not silently expand Release 1. Record it separately and decide whether it belongs in a patch, later chapter, event, or post-launch system.

---

# 2. Release 1 Mission

Release 1 does **not** attempt to ship everything planned for Rascal Realms.

Its purpose is to prove the identity of the game.

By the end of the first meaningful session, the player should understand:

1. Stickerwood is beautiful and worth exploring.
2. The world itself may be deceptive.
3. The World Lies mechanic is fair, readable, and fun.
4. Combat is responsive and stylish.
5. Razz and the creatures have personality.
6. RPG progression gives reasons to continue.
7. Co-op improves the adventure.
8. Crownfall is much bigger than Stickerwood.
9. The game clearly has room to grow through chapters, heroes, pets, mounts, guilds, events, and future realms.

Desired final reaction:

> **“Wait… Stickerwood was only one realm?”**


---

# 3. Golden Scope Rule

> **Never build an entire future feature when a smaller version can establish it today.**

Examples:
- Fishing starts with a few locations, not a giant profession system.
- Pets start with bonding and growth hints, not full mount evolution.
- Guilds exist in worldbuilding before full guild creation.
- Alignment begins through story choices before full factions.
- Future realms are visible before they are playable.
- Mounts begin as animals players can care for.
- Future heroes can arrive through patches instead of blocking launch.

The game should feel larger than the amount of content currently accessible.

# 4. Core Game Pillars

## 4.1 Exploration — LOCKED
Stickerwood must reward curiosity. Players should constantly see things that make them ask:
- What is that?
- Can I reach that?
- Why is that glowing?
- Why does that path look wrong?
- What is behind that waterfall?
- Why is that structure locked?

Exploration rewards may include secrets, caves, NPCs, pets, collectibles, lore, Frauds, suspicious chests, ruins, viewpoints, Crown fragments, and inaccessible future locations visible from afar.

Stickerwood should feel **dense**, not merely large.

## 4.2 The World Lies — LOCKED / SIGNATURE SYSTEM
Possible Frauds include:
- signs
- paths
- bridges
- treasure chests
- NPC statements
- maps
- objective markers
- landmarks
- doors
- enemy disguises
- false shortcuts

### Fairness rule — NON-NEGOTIABLE
> **The world may deceive the player, but the game itself does not cheat.**

Every Fraud must contain readable evidence, preferably at least two clues. UI, tutorials, rules, accessibility information, and system behavior remain reliable. Razz can be wrong; the game cannot be unfair.

## 4.3 Action — LOCKED
Combat comes in peaks:
**exploration → mystery → tension → discovery → action → reward → calm → new mystery**

## 4.4 RPG Progression — APPROVED
Players grow through hero level, abilities, weapons, equipment, relics, pets, exploration, collectibles, and story progression.

## 4.5 Story — LOCKED
Story should be delivered through exploration, dialogue, environmental storytelling, Fraud cases, boss encounters, cinematic moments, discoveries, item lore, and meaningful decisions.

## 4.6 Living World — PLANNED
Sun, moon, clouds, wind, water, rain, birds, insects, wildlife, foliage motion, particles, NPC activity, and ambience make Stickerwood feel alive.

# 7. First 10 Minutes

This sequence requires some of the highest polish in the game.

## 7.1 Crownfall Cinematic
Show the Chaos Crown before the fall. It fractures. Golden energy destabilizes. Violet corruption spreads through distant realms. Do not fully explain the cause.

Goals:
- beauty
- scale
- catastrophe
- mystery

## 7.2 Stickerwood Arrival
Stickerwood first appears warm and desirable.

Normal palette:
- warm green
- wood
- cream
- gold
- sky blue
- sunlight

Crown corruption:
- violet
- magenta
- controlled lime/glitch accents
- fractures
- floating debris
- broken geometry
- distorted signs

Purple signals instability; it should not cover normal Stickerwood.

## 7.3 Razz
Razz is the companion, guide, narrator personality, comic relief, and mystery participant. He is **not playable**.

Razz can make incorrect assumptions. This teaches that characters may be wrong while the game remains fair.

## 7.4 Launch Hero Selection
Release 1 targets three heroes:

### Crown Knight
Relic Sword + Royal Shield\
Accessible melee/frontline hero. Defense, blocking, protection, survivability.

### Glitchcaster
Fracture Staff\
Ranged magic, area effects, Crown-energy spectacle, positioning.

### Shadow Ranger
Whisper Bow\
Precision, mobility, scouting, evasion.

Future canon heroes — Trickster, Lorekeeper, Badge Scout — are deferred to updates.

# 8. First Fraud Encounter

Initial objective:

> **Reach Rascal Plaza**

The player reaches a crossroads. A sign points right.

Possible clues that it is lying:
- footprints continue left
- broken branches point left
- birds react near the real route
- a lantern flickers toward the truth
- the right path loops strangely
- Razz confidently recommends the sign

The player exposes the Fraud.

### Reveal sequence
1. Player confirms suspicion.
2. Sign fractures.
3. Violet Crown energy escapes.
4. False information breaks apart.
5. Real route appears.
6. Cinematic truth UI lands.

This teaches **The World Lies** without a wall of tutorial text.

# 10. Main Story Structure

## Prologue — Crownfall
Chaos Crown fractures. The realms begin changing. Cause remains mysterious.

## Act I — Welcome to Stickerwood
Choose hero, meet Razz, learn movement, explore village, discover first Fraud, reach Rascal Plaza.

## Act II — Something Is Wrong
Introduce Crown Sprouts, Glitch Slimes, Lost Stickers, conflicting NPC information, and early corruption.

## Act III — The Ancient Tree
Reveal deeper history. Stickerwood has an older connection to the Crown; Crownfall effects are not random.

## Act IV — Follow the Wrong Road
Investigation points toward King Wrongway. Frauds become more complex and combat grows more dangerous.

## Act V — Crown Ruins
Major relic/story discovery and the Overgrown Receipt mini-boss.

## Act VI — King Wrongway
Cinematic boss encounter combining combat, deception, clues, route manipulation, and environmental mechanics.

## Epilogue
Wrongway falls. Stickerwood stabilizes. Razz celebrates. The player reaches a high viewpoint. Clouds separate and reveal multiple distant realms and massive Crown fractures.

Then:

# CHAPTER 1 COMPLETE
> **THE CROWNFALL HAS ONLY BEGUN.**

# 47. Final Release Rule

When deciding whether something belongs in Release 1, ask:

> **Does this make Chapter 1 more memorable, or does it only make the feature list longer?**

If it only makes the list longer, defer it.

The player should remember:
- Stickerwood
- Razz
- the first Fraud
- their hero
- their first pet
- their first strange fish
- the Ancient Tree
- King Wrongway
- the final view beyond the clouds

The goal is not to show everything.

The goal is to make players desperate to see what comes next.

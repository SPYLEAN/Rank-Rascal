# Heroes

Six playable heroes. Crown Knight, Glitchcaster, Shadow Ranger and Trickster carry forward their existing `combat`/`field`/`tension` lore from `apps/web/lib/game-content.ts` (**IN DEVELOPMENT**, unchanged in meaning). Lorekeeper and Badge Scout are newly authored for this pass (**CONCEPT**) to match the concept art that already exists for them in the asset pack (`lorekeeper_the_truth_lantern.png`, `crownfall_badge_scout_adventure.png`) — the "Truth Lantern" naming below was picked to match that existing art title.

No hero has final 3D art, a rig, or in-game stats. The hero selector on the homepage is a typographic/color-driven presentation (accent color, icon, role text) — it does not use or imply any character render, because none exists yet for any of the six heroes (the four existing heroes have no portrait art published either).

## Crown Knight — IN DEVELOPMENT
- **Role**: Front-line control and protection.
- **Weapon**: Banner-lance and royal aegis.
- **Powers**: Guard Break Slam · Aegis Anchor (stabilizes unstable objects/routes so the squad can cross or inspect them) · Oath of the Line (draws attention, shields an ally).
- **Mystery specialty**: Reading royal seals and authority markings for forgeries.
- **Quest affinity**: Story, Guild Missions.
- **Tension**: Uses royal technology while questioning the authority that created it.
- **Accent**: Antique Gold / Sky.

## Glitchcaster — IN DEVELOPMENT
- **Role**: Area control and corruption manipulation.
- **Weapon**: Fracture rod.
- **Powers**: Code Fracture Bolt · Rule Invert (temporarily inverts a false environmental rule) · Feedback Pulse (chain-reaction area effect).
- **Mystery specialty**: Reading Crown corruption code fragments other heroes can't see.
- **Quest affinity**: Mysteries, Hidden Quests.
- **Tension**: The most powerful reader of Crown code is also the easiest for the Crown to read back.
- **Accent**: Crown Violet / Hot Magenta.

## Shadow Ranger — IN DEVELOPMENT
- **Role**: Precision damage and traps from changing sightlines.
- **Weapon**: Whisper bow.
- **Powers**: Marked Shot · Trapline · Vanish Step (repositions using terrain, not magic).
- **Mystery specialty**: Physical continuity — prints, broken fibers, wind, disturbed surfaces.
- **Quest affinity**: Bounties.
- **Tension**: Trusts material evidence even when people remember something kinder.
- **Accent**: Forest / Moss.

## Trickster — IN DEVELOPMENT
- **Role**: Mobility, decoys, opportunistic close-range damage.
- **Weapon**: Paired sticker-blades.
- **Powers**: Decoy Double · Rule Break (deliberately breaks a system's assumption to find an edge case) · Opportunist Strike.
- **Mystery specialty**: Testing rules by breaking their assumptions.
- **Quest affinity**: Hidden Quests, Bounties.
- **Tension**: Treats every system as a game until a consequence refuses to reset.
- **Accent**: Hot Magenta / Signal Lime.

## Lorekeeper — CONCEPT
- **Role**: Scholar-adventurer who reconstructs erased history; support and utility, not a front-line fighter.
- **Weapon**: The Unabridged Lantern — a lantern built into a bound book that projects erased text and images onto nearby surfaces.
- **Powers**: Truth Lantern (reveals hidden inscriptions and erased history on a surface) · Marginalia (marks a clue so the whole squad can see it) · Bound Chronicle (briefly restores an erased path or memory for navigation).
- **Mystery specialty**: Erased histories, conflicting written records, contradictory Crown edicts.
- **Quest affinity**: Story, Guild Missions.
- **Tension**: The more history Lorekeeper restores, the more they risk remembering exactly what King Wrongway needs forgotten.
- **Accent**: Paper Cream / Antique Gold.

## Badge Scout — CONCEPT
- **Role**: Resourceful field explorer specializing in gear, traversal tools, and discovery — the completionist archetype.
- **Weapon**: Multi-badge slingpack — a satchel of clip-on tools that convert between gadgets.
- **Powers**: Grapple Badge (traversal pull) · Signal Flare (marks a discovery for the squad) · Toolkit Swap (adapts loadout to solve an environmental puzzle on the fly).
- **Mystery specialty**: Hidden discoveries, secret passages, environmental puzzle mechanisms.
- **Quest affinity**: Hidden Quests, Bounties.
- **Tension**: Collects "badges" — trophies of solved lies — obsessively, and struggles with which discoveries are worth risking the squad for.
- **Accent**: Signal Lime / Sky.

> Naming note: a hero's in-fiction "Badges" (Badge Scout's trophies) are a distinct future in-game Roblox collectible concept, unrelated to the real, currently-live Discord bot badges (Quest Crusader, Drip Monarch, Veteran Noob). See `CONFLICT_REPORT.md`.

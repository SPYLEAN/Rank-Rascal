# World

Eleven locations across the connected realm of Stickerwood. Starting Village, Mystery Forest, Glitch Grove and Crown Ruins are the existing Episode 1 districts (**IN DEVELOPMENT** — full lore already in `EPISODE_ONE_BEATS`/`STICKERWOOD_REGIONS`). The remaining seven are newly authored for this pass (**CONCEPT** or **PLANNED**), consistent with the established tone, `CORE_LOOP`, and `LORE_ERAS`.

Stickerwood is one connected realm, not four disconnected dioramas — the map should read as one place with distinct districts, per `ART_DIRECTION.md`.

## Starting Village — IN DEVELOPMENT
The festival of forced cheer. Every signboard insists the roads are safe; a courier vanishes on the "safest" route anyway. **Quest styles**: Story.

## Stickerwood (the Heartwood) — CONCEPT
The literal ancient grove the whole realm is named for — distinct from "Stickerwood" the realm-wide name. Layered paper-craft trees etched with the realm's oldest, half-legible treaties. **Mysteries**: a missing page from the founding treaty; carved names that rearrange overnight. **Threats**: whispering Crown echoes repeating contradictory oaths. **Discoveries**: a growth-ring timeline showing the realm is far older than the Crown's official history admits. **Notable characters**: elder Lost Stickers who remember Stickerwood before the Crown. **Quest styles**: Story, Hidden Quests.

## Mystery Forest — IN DEVELOPMENT
Ancient trail markers survive beneath newer bark; wind, compass anomalies and Lost Stickers preserve testimony the village won't acknowledge. **Quest styles**: Mysteries.

## Ancient Tree — CONCEPT
A single colossal world-tree that physically links districts. **Mysteries**: growth rings out of sync with recorded history; a trunk-door that opens only to a correct accusation. **Threats**: glitch-root tangles that reroute travelers. **Discoveries**: a hollow chamber archive. **Notable characters**: the Rootbound Archivist, a Lost Sticker who guards the hollow. **Quest styles**: Mysteries, Guild Missions.

## Glitch Grove — IN DEVELOPMENT
Fractured islands obeying mutually exclusive Crown commands; combat and traversal become one deduction. **Quest styles**: Mysteries, Bounties.

## Rascal Plaza — PLANNED
A market square where villagers trade rumors — the in-fiction counterpart to the real Rascal Labs community. **Mysteries**: contradicting rumor boards; a merchant who insists he's met the squad before. **Threats**: minor Crown Sprout pickpockets exploiting crowd confusion. **Discoveries**: side bounty postings and an in-fiction Founders Guild recruitment board. **Quest styles**: Bounties, Guild Missions.

> Naming note: this is a distinct in-game location from the real "Rascal Plaza" community banner already on the site (`CommunityPlazaSection.tsx`). See `CONFLICT_REPORT.md`.

## River Path — PLANNED
A waterway between village and forest, contested by conflicting "official" water-level records. **Mysteries**: a bridge on no two matching maps; fish swimming against a current the maps insist doesn't exist. **Threats**: sudden false floods engineered by a Crown correction. **Discoveries**: a message in a bottle from a courier who "never left." **Quest styles**: Story, Bounties.

## Hidden Cove — PLANNED
A secluded inlet used by Lost Stickers as a hideout. **Mysteries**: a cove on old maps but not new ones; footsteps with no visible owner. **Threats**: submerged glitch fissures. **Discoveries**: a cache of pre-Crown artifacts. **Quest styles**: Hidden Quests.

## Crown Ruins — IN DEVELOPMENT
The buried royal control apparatus; King Wrongway's last unedited decree waits above the realm. **Quest styles**: Story (Act IV climax).

## Sky Bridges — CONCEPT
Floating causeways linking Glitch Grove to Crown Ruins — a natural extension of Glitch Grove's contradictory-gravity mechanic. **Mysteries**: bridges that exist only while a specific rule is believed true. **Threats**: inversion gales. **Discoveries**: a suspended shrine holding a discarded royal decree. **Quest styles**: Mysteries, Guild Missions.

## King Wrongway Citadel — PLANNED
The true seat beyond Crown Ruins — sealed, not enterable in the current Episode 1 design. A future-chapter destination. **Mysteries**: the sealed throne room; Wrongway's "one perfect road" blueprint. **Threats**: defenses that activate only once a lie is fully exposed elsewhere in the realm. **Discoveries**: none yet — deliberately sealed. **Quest styles**: Story (future).
